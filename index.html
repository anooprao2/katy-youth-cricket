About KYC

More than runs
and wickets

Katy Youth Cricket is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit run by parent volunteers. What started in 2013 with four kids has become a cornerstone of youth cricket in the Greater Houston area.

Our professional coaches lead five age groups, and every session combines cricket skills with character. We want players to leave KYC with integrity, discipline and teamwork, along with a good cover drive.

Character

Integrity, discipline and resilience, built one session at a time.

Community

Families, volunteers and players who show up for each other.

Cricket

Professional coaching and competitive play at every level.

"Everyone has two hands, one to help yourself and the second to help others."
